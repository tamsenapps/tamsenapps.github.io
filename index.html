Autopost: privacy notice

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Autopost is a private tool. It has one user, its owner. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and posts to the owner's own social media Pages, at times the owner has chosen.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Autopost asks for one permission: to manage the owner's YouTube videos. It uses that permission only to upload the owner's videos and thumbnails. It does not read private data from any other person.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's credential store. Nothing is sent to any third party. No analytics, cookies or advertising are used on this page or in the tool.

Sharing and retention

The tool does not collect, sell, share or keep personal information about anyone else. Removing the tool's access in the owner's Google Account settings ends all access immediately.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the owner of this website, through the contact details on the main site.
